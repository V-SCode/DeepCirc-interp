**Table S1. Maximum circuit-score designs.** Fifteen designs, one per target
function, ranked by predicted circuit score. Circuit and growth scores are
shown on their raw scales. Size denotes the number of regulators. Parts are
listed from input-proximal to output-adjacent positions; arrows indicate this
ordering, not necessarily a direct connection between adjacent parts. Colored
names identify the five most frequent parts across the 30 selected entries in
Tables S1 and S2: PhlF/P2 (orange), QacR/Q2 (rose), BetI/E1 (light blue),
AmtR/A1 (green), and SrpR/S4 (blue). Scores are MLP predictions.

**Table S2. Pareto-optimal designs and recurrent parts.** Fifteen Pareto-knee
designs, one per target function, ranked by combined score. The combined score
is the sum of normalized log₂ circuit score and normalized growth score;
the circuit and growth columns show raw MLP predictions. Size, part ordering,
and part colors follow Table S1. The lower block reports occurrences of the
five most frequent parts in Table S1 (15 entries), Table S2 (15 entries), and
their combined set (30 entries). Counts refer to selected entries; the same
part assignment can recur for different target functions.

**Table S3. Circuit-score enrichment by part and cascade position.** Mean
log₂ enrichment in the top 5% relative to the bottom 5% of designs ranked by
circuit score, grouped by part, gate type (NOT or NOR), and position. Position
is defined by depth to output: early, ≥3; middle, 2; late, 1 (output-adjacent).
Positive values indicate enrichment and negative values indicate depletion.
Cells use a gray–white–blue scale centered on zero and saturated at −3 and +3.
AmeR/F1 at NOT–late has a value of −7.33; only its color is saturated, and all
printed values are unchanged. Rows retain the source analysis ordering.
